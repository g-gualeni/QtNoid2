- QtNoid::App::Settings::appExeOrAppBundleDirPath();

The first one return the execution of the app bundle folder (macOS).

New classes for QtNoidApp:
    Config
    ConfigFile
    ConfigGlobal
    Development

New library:
- QtNoidJson
